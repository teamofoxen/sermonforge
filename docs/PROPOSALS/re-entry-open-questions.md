> **Status: OPEN QUESTIONS, no build authorized.** Recorded in the Session-6

A pastor preaches ~42 weeks a year and preps across a week — but life
interrupts, and a sermon can sit untouched for days. What the tool owes him
at that return is not yet ruled:

1. **What does the pastor actually need after 48–72 hours away?** A
   re-reading of his own last work? The passage again? Something else?
   Unknown until observed.
2. **What happens when interpretation changes after the Outline or Body
   already exists?** Today: nothing invalidates downstream work, by design
   (movement is monotonic in expectation, not enforcement). Is silent
   staleness ever a trust problem in practice, or does the pastor naturally
   re-walk what matters?
3. **Does he need a "where this sermon stands" summary on re-entry?** The
   Finish screen answers "is it done"; the map answers "what's answered";
   neither is framed as "here's where you left off and why."
4. **Are the existing surfaces — the Map, the reference pane's "Your work"
   tab, and the Study → Anchor handoff — already sufficient?** They may be;
   the honest answer requires watching a real multi-day prep, not
   speculation.

**Evidence source when it comes:** the pastor's own lived prep (the standing
"lived sermon prep validates" thread) and BTI-cohort feedback. Until then
this stays a note.
